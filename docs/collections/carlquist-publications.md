# Sherwin Carlquist Publications Dataset

Draft, generated 2026-11-17 from the dataset README and `fwbg_collection_data/docs/sources/carlquist_publications.md`.

## What it is
Bibliographic metadata for the scientific publications of Sherwin Carlquist, a botanist known for plant anatomy, wood evolution and island biology. Columns follow CSL-JSON and Dublin Core; Wikidata IDs are included for all publications and journals and a subset of co-authors. Authors of the dataset: Jason H. Best and Amanda Gomez.
